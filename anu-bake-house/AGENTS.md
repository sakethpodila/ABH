# Application guidance

Read `../AGENTS.md`, then `docs/PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`, `docs/STATUS.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, and `docs/DECISIONS.md` before making changes. Those documents are the shared context across chats.

The current approved slice is local menu management: list, add/edit, save/reopen, preview, image uploads, manual tags, form/list publish actions, and public category/tag filters. Discount rules, other workflows, and online deployment require further review. Customers have no accounts or checkout. Do not deploy or provision paid resources. Keep STATUS.md and decision records current after substantive changes.
