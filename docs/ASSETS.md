# Asset provenance

`public/images/logo.jpg` and `anu.png` are copies of the user's attached logo and mascot. Ten other images were collected from the public grid at https://www.instagram.com/anu_sbakehouse/ on 2026-11-07, for this business's website.

`public/images/candles/` contains numbered candles 1–6 for workspace navigation. Number 2 is the user's supplied replacement image; the earlier supplied number 2 is retained there as `candle-2.png`. Its original source and reuse rights are not confirmed; review before launch.
`docs/source-assets/candle-sheet.svg` is a copy of the user's supplied `ABH-desktop-pages.svg`. The numbers 1, 3, 4, 5, and 6 were cropped from that SVG into transparent PNGs in the candles folder. No new artwork was invented.

`public/images/cake-cursor.png` is a small transparent cursor asset generated from the user's supplied cake-slice image. The original image was reoriented into an upright pointer, so fine illustration details may differ from the attachment.
`public/images/candle-cursor.png` is a transparent cursor asset generated to match the cake pointer and the user's pink candle reference. It is used over editable text fields.

The cake logo is used for the favicon and public/private brand links; the mom mascot appears in the homepage kitchen introduction. Original image files remain unchanged; borders, sizing, and rounded corners are CSS presentation only.
